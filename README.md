# Global Auto Sales Dashboard
## Project Overview
This project is an interactive sales dashboard built in Microsoft Excel to analyze global automobile sales performance. The dashboard provides insights into sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, and revenue distribution, enabling data-driven business decisions.

## Business Questions Answered
* What is the total revenue generated?
* How many units were sold?
* Which product lines generate the highest sales?
* Who are the top-performing customers?
* Which countries contribute the most sales?
* How are sales distributed across deal sizes?
* What is the monthly sales trend?
* What is the distribution of order statuses?
## Key Metrics
* Total Revenue: $9.76M
* Total Unit Solds: 96,428
* Total Orders: 2,747
* Average Order Value: $3,553
## Key Insights 
### Product Performance 
* Classic Cars recorded the highest sales volume.
* Vintage Cars ranked second among product categories.
### Geographic Performance 
* The USA generated the highest sales.
* Spain and France followed as strong-performing markets.
### Customer Analysis 
* Euro Shopping Channel emerged as the top customer by sales.
* Mini Gifts Distributors Ltd. ranked second.
### Sales Trend 
* Sales peaked significantly in November.
* Mid-year sales experienced a temporary decline before recovering.
